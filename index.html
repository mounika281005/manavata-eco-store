More than a store.
A movement towards better living.

Manavata is an international voluntary, not-for-profit organisation working towards creating a Healthy, Happy and Harmonious world.

Through initiatives in holistic health, value education, yoga, spirituality and environmental sustainability, Manavata inspires individuals and communities to embrace healthier and more sustainable ways of living.

The Manavata Organic Store brings together natural products, traditional knowledge and sustainable living choices.
